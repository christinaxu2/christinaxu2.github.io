Across the Aisle Episode 1: Kavanaugh

Across the Aisle Episode 1

On the first episode of Across the Aisle, Andrew and Taylor are joined by Cameron Koffman, the president of the William F. Buckley, Jr. Program and Ananya Kumar-Banerjee, the communications director of the Yale Dems, to discuss the confirmation of Brett Kavanaugh, its impact on Yale, and its implications for the upcoming midterm elections.

In Photos: Solidarity with Survivors, a Rally for a Better Yale

Presenting snapshots of last week’s Solidarity with Survivors march and rally

On September 26th, hundreds of Yale undergraduate students marched from the Old Campus to the Women’s Table chanting “Yale’s complicit, that we know! Kavanaugh has got to go!” Students demanded the administration (including Dean Marvin Chun, who attended the rally) condemn Supreme Court nominee Brett Kavanaugh ’87 YLS ’90. Beyond the political struggle, students also wanted the University to take drastic actions to rectify Yale’s culture and history that they believe allows misogynists to thrive.

Sacred Ceilings, Consumerist Key Chains: The Vatican’s Religious Art and Tourist Culture

Vatican City, the spiritual heart of the Roman Catholic Church, is revered as one of the holiest places on earth. Yet, upon entering its thousand-year-old walls, rampant tourism and consumerism seem to dilute the sense of spirituality the city is designed to evoke. It begs the question: How can spaces remain sacred in an increasingly capitalistic and consumerist society?

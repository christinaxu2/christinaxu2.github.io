Trump’s Turkey Pardon Liveblog

The Politic provides live coverage of the first Turkey Pardon of the Trump Presidency.

Welcome to The Politic ‘s liveblog coverage of President Trump’s first Turkey Pardon. Please join Trent Kannegieter ’21, Kate Kushner ’21, and Megan McQueen ’20 as they comment on the event .

Trent Kannegieter is a first-year in Saybrook who hasn’t stopped stuffing himself with barbecue since getting back home to Alabama on Sunday night.

Kate Kushner is a first-year in Pauli Murray participating in The Politic ‘s Staff Writer program. She is from North Carolina and enjoys exploring Yale’s libraries and eating spicy food.

Megan McQueen is a sophomore in Pierson studying Statistics and Data Science. She is currently Online Managing Editor of The Politic.

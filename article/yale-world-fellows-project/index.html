Yale World Fellows, 2013

Yale World Fellows Project

The Politic interviews the 2013 class of Yale World Fellows.

Every year, Yale University selects a class of World Fellows – rising global leaders from diverse disciplines and backgrounds – for a four month, on-campus residency program. The Politic interviewed the full Class of 2013 about their international leadership experiences.
